# Obsidian Sync Guide

If you're using Obsidian across multiple devices and want everything to sync up smoothly, you have several options to choose from. This guide will help you understand the different approaches and choose the best one for your needs.

## Option 1: Obsidian Sync (Recommended)

The easiest and most integrated solution is **Obsidian Sync**. This is Obsidian's own subscription service at **$10/month**, designed to handle everything seamlessly:

- ✅ All your notes sync automatically
- ✅ Settings sync across devices
- ✅ Plugins sync automatically
- ✅ Secure end-to-end encryption
- ✅ Version history included
- ✅ No manual configuration needed

**Best for:** Users who want the most hassle-free approach and don't mind the monthly subscription.

## Option 2: iCloud Drive

If you prefer not to pay for Obsidian Sync, you can use **iCloud Drive** to sync your vault:

### Setup Steps:
1. Store your vault folder directly in your iCloud Drive
2. Enable iCloud Drive on all devices you're using
3. Ensure you're logged into the same iCloud account on all devices
4. Verify iCloud Drive settings are enabled for Obsidian

### Troubleshooting:
- **Sync issues?** Check that all devices are fully logged into the same iCloud account
- **Missing files?** Verify iCloud Drive settings are enabled on each device
- **Conflicts?** Make sure iCloud Drive has finished syncing before making edits

**Best for:** Apple ecosystem users who already use iCloud Drive and prefer not to pay for additional subscriptions.

## Option 3: Dropbox

Another alternative is using **Dropbox** to sync your vault:

### Setup Steps:
1. Place your vault folder in your Dropbox directory
2. Install Dropbox on all your devices
3. Wait for initial sync to complete before opening the vault on multiple devices

### Considerations:
- Works across multiple platforms (Windows, Mac, Linux, mobile)
- May require manual conflict resolution if you edit on multiple devices simultaneously
- Dropbox's free tier has storage limitations

**Best for:** Users who already have a Dropbox subscription or prefer a platform-agnostic solution.

## Summary

- **Most hassle-free:** Obsidian Sync subscription ($10/month)
- **Apple users:** iCloud Drive (requires careful setup)
- **Cross-platform:** Dropbox (requires some manual tweaking)

All three options work well, but Obsidian Sync is designed specifically for this purpose and will give you the smoothest experience. If you're comfortable with a bit of manual setup, iCloud or Dropbox can also do the trick—it just might take a bit more tweaking to get everything working perfectly.
